Core Features

Automated Test Generation

Generate randomized, print-ready exams from your question bank in seconds. Each student gets a unique version with QR-coded answer sheets.

Automated Correction

Photograph completed answer sheets and let the OMR engine grade them instantly. Review, correct by hand, and publish results to students with one click.

Question Bank

Build a reusable pool of questions categorized by topic and difficulty, shared across courses and exams.
